What AI does and does not do

AI does

  • Asks staff the right questions, step by step
  • Puts the case together for the doctor
  • Drafts notes and orders for the record
  • Sends reminders after the call
  • Writes the message for the family
  • Counts calls for the monthly report

AI does not

  • Diagnose
  • Choose or change treatment
  • Decide to keep someone or send them to hospital
  • Replace the doctor or the nurse

Rules we follow

Telesora is in development. These are the rules we are building it for.

  • Medical devices (MDR)

    Software can count as a medical device depending on what it is meant to do. We are designing Telesora’s AI to organise, document and remind, not to diagnose or choose treatment. Some parts, such as the danger-sign rules in the staff questions, may still count as medical device software. Before launch, regulatory experts will assess each part, and any part that is a medical device will follow the Medical Device Regulation.

    EU guidance on medical device software (MDCG 2019-11)

  • Health data (GDPR)

    Health data is a special category of personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. It needs a clear legal basis and strong protection. Before the pilot we will carry out a data protection impact assessment.

    GDPR on EUR-Lex

  • Artificial intelligence (AI Act)

    Since 2 August 2026, the EU AI Act requires that people are told when they deal with an AI system. Everything the AI writes in Telesora will be clearly marked as a draft. If a part of Telesora counts as a medical device that needs an outside check, the AI Act treats that part as high-risk, with extra duties.

    AI Act on EUR-Lex

  • Telemedicine

    Spain has no single telemedicine law: health services are largely regulated by each region, and rules also differ between countries. A health law specialist will review the service before the pilot.

Built for busy hands

  • Big buttons, few words

    Night staff are busy and not always used to apps. One tap starts a call for help.

  • Protected calls and records

    We are designing for encrypted video and data, with access only for the people who need it.

  • Consent before messages

    Families get messages only if the resident or their representative agrees.

  • Doctors licensed in Spain

    On-call doctors will hold a licence to practise in Spain, our own or through a medical partner.
