How a night call works

From the first worry to the morning report, in seven steps.

Everyone sees what they need

  1. Opens the app on the tablet
  2. Picks the situation with one tap
  3. Answers short questions, measures, takes a photo
  4. Joins the video call
  5. Gets clear reminders after the call
  1. Gets the case before the call starts
  2. Sees illnesses, medicines and today’s changes
  3. Talks to staff and sees the resident
  4. Confirms the notes AI has drafted
  5. Is alerted if the resident gets worse
  1. Gives consent once
  2. Gets a short, plain message
  3. Knows what happened and what was done
  4. Can ask the care home for more
  1. Sees how many calls there were
  2. Sees how many hospital trips were avoided
  3. Has every note in the medical record
  4. Can show families how nights are covered

Safety comes first

  • Questions written by doctors

    Every question for staff will be written and checked by doctors.

  • Danger signs mean 112

    If the answers show danger signs, the app says to call 112 at once.

  • The doctor always decides

    AI never chooses a treatment and never sends anyone home or to hospital.

  • Notes are checked

    Nothing goes into the medical record until the doctor confirms it.

We start with four night situations

Fever, falls, sudden confusion and shortness of breath. These are the moments when staff most need a doctor at night.
