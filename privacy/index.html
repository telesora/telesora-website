Privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The person responsible (data controller) is Liubov Kniahynytska, founder of Telesora. The company is not registered yet: registration is under way. Once it is registered, the company will take over this role and we will add its name, address and tax number here.

Contact for any privacy question: info@telesora.com

This website in short

No cookies. No analytics or tracking. Nothing is loaded from other companies: fonts and images come from our own website. The contact form sends nothing by itself.

When you visit the website

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. To deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of your visit, the page you asked for and your browser type. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cloudflare acts as our data processor under its data processing agreement. It may process data outside the European Union. Such transfers are protected by the safeguards in that agreement, such as the EU standard contractual clauses.

Your language choice

When you pick a language, your browser stores that choice on your device (local storage), so the site opens in your language next time. It is never sent to us. This storage is strictly necessary for a function you asked for, so it needs no consent. You can delete it in your browser settings at any time.

The contact form

The form does not send anything to us or to anyone else, and nothing you type is stored on this website. When you press Send, it only prepares an email in your own mail app. We receive only what you decide to send.

Emails you send us

What we use: your name, email address, country, topic and message, and anything else you choose to write.

Why: to answer you and, if you wish, to talk about a pilot, work or a partnership.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) and steps you ask us to take before a possible agreement (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Who else sees it: only our email provider, which stores the mail for us as a data processor. Its name will be added here before launch.

How long we keep it: as long as the conversation needs, and no longer than 12 months after your last message, unless an agreement follows or the law requires us to keep it longer.

Please do not send health details

This website is not a medical service. Please do not send us details about anyone’s health by email.

No automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions or profiles about you from your visit or your emails.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, limit its use, object to its use and get a copy in a common format (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect what was done before. Write to the address above.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to a data protection authority: in Spain the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), or the authority in the country where you live.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when the company is registered and before the Telesora service starts. The date at the top shows the current version.
